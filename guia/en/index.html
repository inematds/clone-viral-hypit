In short

This is a free INEMA kit, in Portuguese, with ready-made messages to paste into Claude Code, file templates, checklists and a price table. It teaches you to use Hypit, an open video tool, to remake a short clip with your own face, make a version in another language, or swap the person for fictional characters. It suits anyone in marketing or content creation who wants to test several versions of an ad without filming again. You need Claude Code, a fal account with a few dollars of credit, a clip of yours and two photos of your face.

What it is

Three pieces, one kit (in Portuguese)

Hypit plans and assembles the video on your computer. fal runs the image and video models. Claude Code talks to you and writes the files. This kit gathers what you need to tell it.

🧠 You ask in text

Four ready-made messages, to paste into Claude Code. No commands to type.

💵 Price before spending

Every message ends with "Show me the plan and the price and don't build anything." Claude quotes the cost and stops.

🧩 One base, many versions

After the first one, a new face or a new language is just one more message, not a day of filming.

How it works

The 3 steps

Install, remake with your face, check and make versions. At each one, Claude shows the plan and the price first.

1 · Install Hypit + fal→ 2 · Remake with your face→ 3 · Check, fix and make versions

1. Install

One message installs Hypit in the project only, connects fal by reading FAL_KEY from the environment and points to the Chrome you already have. About 6 minutes.

2. Remake

Hypit takes the first and last frame of the clip, puts your face in both and generates the 8 seconds between them. Images first, video after.

3. Check and make versions

Face, hands and text in the scene. Fix with premium models. Then: another language or fictional characters in the same clip.

Prerequisites

What you need

Four things, plus Chrome. Claude checks the rest (such as Node.js 22.15+) and warns you if something is missing.

Claude Code

Runs on your Claude plan. Let it ask permission before running commands; no automatic mode for paid work.

fal account with credit

A few dollars are enough to start. Create the API key in the fal.ai dashboard and keep it in your terminal profile:

# at the end of ~/.zshrc (Mac) or ~/.bashrc (Linux)
export FAL_KEY="paste-your-key-here"

# check without showing the value
[ -n "$FAL_KEY" ] && echo "FAL_KEY loaded"

Never paste the key into the chat. Hypit reads it from your computer.

A short clip of yours

Preferably under 10 seconds. Yours, or one you have the right to remake.

Two sharp photos of your face

One facing the camera and one slightly turned. No sunglasses, no hand over the mouth, with good light.

User guide · step by step

From clip to versions

Paste each message into Claude Code, in the project folder. The full versions, with explanations, are in kit/ (in Portuguese).

1

Set up the folder 🧠 you

An empty folder for the project, with the clip and the photos inside references/.

meu-clone/
└── references/
    ├── meu-clipe.mp4
    ├── rosto-1.jpg
    └── rosto-2.jpg
2

Install Hypit and connect fal 🧠 you · ⚙️ Hypit

Open Claude Code in that folder and paste the installation message. Common gotchas: the package is @hypit/hypit; the skill goes into the project only (npx skills add hypit-ai/hypit, without -g); npm pkg set needs --json to save true as a boolean.

Install Hypit in this folder. Install the Hypit skill from github.com/hypit-ai/hypit for this project only, and the hypit command through npm. Then connect fal so the images and clips run on fal: Hypit doesn't ship with fal, so write a small project Provider package in packages/provider-fal that reads my FAL_KEY from the environment and never saves the key to a file. FAL_KEY is already set. Point Hypit's page renderer to the Chrome I already have installed. Show me the plan first and then check that everything works.
3

Remake the clip with your face 🧠 you · 🤖 fal

First and last frame with your face, then the video between them. In two rounds, so you can check the images before paying for the video. About US$0.71 by the 10/21/2026 price table.

/hypit Remake the first 8 seconds of references/meu-clipe.mp4 with my face, using references/rosto-1.jpg and references/rosto-2.jpg. Take the first and last frame of the clip and put my face in both, keeping the same pose, the same room and the same light. Then make the 8-second clip between those two frames. Split it into two rounds, images first and then the clip, so I can check the images before paying for the video. Show me the plan and the price and don't build anything.
Why the last sentence is the most important. "Show me the plan and the price and don't build anything." turns the request into a quote: Claude reads, plans, calculates the cost on fal and stops. You check whether it understood the clip, whether the models and resolution are the ones you asked for, and whether the price fits. Only then do you answer "go ahead and build". If the price comes as an estimate, ask it to check on the fal pages.
4

Check 3 things 🧠 you

The face (eyes, nose, smile), the hands (count the fingers in fast movements) and any text in the scene — a "CEREAL" sign can turn into "CENLIL". In versions with characters, also check hair and teeth.

5

Fix with premium models 🧠 you · 🤖 fal

A stronger image model, a middle frame and 1080p video. Same prompts, only the model changes. About US$2.

/hypit The face and the text in the scene look fake. Redo it with premium models: GPT Image 2.5 at maximum quality for three frames (start, middle and end), so the motion doesn't go off track, and MiniMax H3 Max at 1080p for two clips of about 4 seconds each. Keep all the prompts the same, changing only the model. Show me the plan and the price and don't build anything.
6

Make the version in another language 🧠 you · 🤖 fal

Translates and trims the speech, new voice, check with Whisper, lips synced and subtitles. If the voice gets a number or a rare word wrong, write it the way it is spoken and check again. About US$2.

/hypit Make a Japanese version of my premium clip. Translate the speech and trim it to fit in 8 seconds, make a new voice, run a speech check, sync the lips to the new voice and add subtitles. Show me the plan and the price and don't build anything.
7

One clip, several fictional people 🧠 you · 🤖 fal

With the kit's characters.json (made-up characters). If the safety filter blocks a photo (content_policy_violation), soften that character's description and rerun only that one.

/hypit I want an 8-second UGC clip remade with different characters, one version per character in characters.json. Each character gets an identity photo made by GPT Image 2.5, and then MiniMax H3 Recast puts that person into the clip at 1080p, keeping the movements, the camera and the timing. Show me the plan and the total price of all the versions and don't build anything.
8

Test the versions in an ad 🧠 you

Run them all as a single test, with the same small budget for each. After a few days, keep the two that the audience watches the longest and make new versions of the winner: face, setting or language.

The 3 mistakes that make everything look fake

Clip too long

Stay under 10 seconds. Recast accepts 5 to 30 s, but short gets it right more often and costs less.

Bad face photo

No sunglasses, no hand over the mouth. One facing the camera, one turned, good light.

Video before images

Check the images (cents) before paying for the clip (dollars).

Ethics and license

The rule before everything

Use only your own face, or a face you have written permission to use. Use only clips of your own, or clips you have the right to remake. No celebrities, no coworkers without authorization, no videos made by others. The kit's characters are fictional. If the ad looks like a testimonial, say that it is AI-generated content.

Hypit license (summary)

  • Free to use in your work and your clients' work, including commercial.
  • No hosted or multi-tenant service for third parties.
  • No selling or packaging Hypit (or a derivative).
  • Keep the Hypit name and logo.

Read the full license in the Hypit repository. That is why Hypit is not included in this kit.
